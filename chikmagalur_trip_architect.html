Chikmagalur Coffee & Cloud Peaks

⏳ Sep 12–14, 2026 • 2–3 Travelers • Tresca Basecamp
Day 1 • Saturday, Sep 12

Waterfalls, Cloud Walks & Sunset

🚖 Private Cab + 4x4 Jeep
09:30 AM – 10:15 AM

Arrival & Strategic Bag Drop at Tresca Hotel

Arrive at KSRTC Stand. Take a 2-min auto (850m) to Tresca A Luxury Hotel. Drop bags at 24-hr reception cloakroom, freshen up in lobby washroom, and change into comfortable hill clothes.

💡 Strategy: Standard check-in is 12 PM. Head straight out without carrying bags!
10:15 AM – 10:45 AM

South Indian Breakfast & Filter Coffee

Quick crispy dosas and piping hot Malnad filter coffee at Vishnu Delicacy or Town Caffee (right along RG Road, 2 min walk).

11:00 AM Pickup Cab Pickup

Private Sightseeing Cab Pickup

Your pre-arranged private cab picks you up directly at Tresca to comfortably tackle the mountain hairpin curves.

11:45 AM – 01:45 PM

Jhari (Buttermilk) Falls Off-Road 4x4 Jeep

Cab drops you at the coffee estate checkpost. Switch to local 4x4 open jeep (~₹800 per jeep) for a thrilling, muddy off-road ride down into private coffee plantations to the roaring falls.

🌊 Gear: Wear grippy footwear. Rocks near the spray can be slippery.
02:00 PM – 03:00 PM

Countryside Malnad Lunch

Stop at Siri Cafe or a traditional Malnad outlet along the route for hearty local lunch before the peak climb.

03:30 PM – 05:30 PM

Mullayanagiri Peak (Highest in Karnataka, 1,930m)

Late afternoon timing is prime: morning tourist congestion has cleared. Climb the stone steps along the windy ridge to the highest point in Karnataka for panoramic views through drifting mist.

⛰️ Weather: High winds and sudden chills. Keep light windcheaters zipped.
05:45 PM – 06:45 PM

Hirekolale Lake Sunset

Stop by on the way down from the hills. Sit on the stone embankment watching the tranquil sunset reflecting across the mountain waters.

07:15 PM onwards

Formal Room Check-in & RG Road Dinner

Cab drops back at Tresca. Complete formal room check-in, unpack, hot shower, followed by dinner at The Estate Cafe or along RG Road (Kailash Parbat / Hotel Matsya).

Day 2 • Sunday, Sep 13

Ridge Views & Plantation Cruising

🛵 125cc Scooty Freedom Day
09:30 AM

Collect 125cc Scooty near KSRTC Bus Stand

Walk 500m to rental hub (Happy Go Rentals / Royal Brothers). Collect 125cc scooter.

🛵 Crucial Rule: If 2 travelers: rent 1 scooty (125cc). If 3 travelers: rent 2 scooties to ensure mountain uphill power and avoid heavy traffic police fines on ghat turns.
10:00 AM – 01:30 PM

Baba Budangiri & Z-Point Ridge Trail

Ride up scenic ghat roads through thick coffee estates towards Baba Budangiri. Take the trail towards Z-Point (Kemmanagundi)—a thrilling narrow ridge walk with lush green valley drops on either side.

03:30 PM – 05:30 PM

Coffee Board Museum & Cafe Pour-Overs

Ride back along KM Road to visit Coffee Museum (Coffee Board of India) to learn coffee harvesting, roasting, and bean classification. Stop by Cafe Agape or World of Coffee for artisanal pour-overs.

Day 3 • Monday, Sep 14

Heritage, Coffee Shopping & Departure

🏛️ Belur Temple + Sleeper Bus
11:00 AM

Check-out & Luggage Cloakroom Hold

Check out of Tresca. Hand backpacks to reception to safely hold in cloakroom for the entire day.

11:30 AM – 03:00 PM

Belur Chennakeshava Temple (Option A) or Aldur Canopy (Option B)

• Option A: 25 km highway scooty ride to Belur to explore the 12th-century Chennakeshava Temple (intricate Hoysala soapstone carvings).
• Option B: Scenic cruise on Aldur/Mudigere road under canopy trees with a stop at Siri Coffee sculpture gardens.

05:00 PM – 07:30 PM

Coffee & Spice Shopping on MG Road

• Fresh Ground Coffee: Freshly roasted Arabica/Robusta blends at Panduranga Coffee or Classic Coffee.
• Malnad Spices: Whole green cardamom, black pepper, and cinnamon bark.
• Local Snacks: Hot banana and jackfruit chips.

10:15 PM – 11:30 PM

Board Overnight AC Sleeper Bus to BLR

Walk/auto to KSRTC Stand. Board 10:30 PM AC Sleeper bus back to Bengaluru. Arrives Tuesday ~05:00 AM.

Tresca A Luxury Hotel

RG Road, Vijayapura (Opp. Renukacharya Kalyana Mantapa)

9:30 AM Arrival Strategy

Because you arrive only 2.5 hours before formal check-in (12:00 PM), head straight to Tresca. Request early check-in if rooms are ready. If still occupied, drop bags at the 24-hr cloakroom, freshen up in the lobby washroom, and head out immediately for breakfast and sightseeing without carrying any luggage!

Dining & Kitchen Note

Tresca features clean, modern rooms but does not have a full in-house restaurant kitchen. Because it is located right on RG Road, top-tier dining options (Vishnu Delicacy, Kailash Parbat, Town Caffee, Hotel Matsya) are within a 2- to 5-minute easy walk.

Bus Stand Proximity

Just 850 meters (~2 minutes by auto / 8 minutes walk) from the KSRTC Main Bus Stand.

Chikmagalur Coffee Roasters

Where to buy fresh authentic blends

Panduranga Coffee (MG Road)

Operating since 1938. The gold standard for Chikmagalur coffee. Get whole roasted beans or have them grind custom blends (80:20 Arabica:Chicory or 100% Pure Malnad Peaberry) right in front of you for your specific brewing method.

Classic Coffee & Coffee Board

Single-estate premium Arabicas with distinctive chocolate and fruit tasting notes. Visit the Coffee Museum on KM Road to understand the wet pulping process.

Malnad Spices & Snacks

Fresh plantation harvest checklist

Estate Spices Checklist

  • Green Cardamom: Look for whole, vibrant green 8mm pods with dense aroma.
  • Black Pepper: Authentic Malnad sun-dried Tellicherry grade peppercorns.
  • Cinnamon: True Ceylon / Malnad rolled bark.
  • Hot Chips: Pick up freshly fried salted banana and jackfruit chips on Market Road.

Scooty Rental Guidelines

• Pickup Point: Walk 500m to KSRTC Stand / Barlane Road (Happy Go Rentals or Royal Brothers).

• Engine Power: Insist on 125cc (Activa 125 / Jupiter 125) for climbing steep hill ghats.

• Group Size Rule: If 2 travelers = 1 scooty. If 3 travelers = rent 2 scooties to avoid triple-riding fines (~₹1,500) and dangerous mountain stalling.

• Return Time: Monday by 08:30 PM to settle deposit before boarding night sleeper bus.
