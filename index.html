How Multiple Trips Are Managed

Clean, scalable, offline-first architecture

1. Master Hub (`index.html`)

The Hub is your master landing deck. It presents high-level trip cards, upcoming countdowns, stats, and a quick preview drawer. It connects all your journeys in one unified portal.

2. Dedicated Trip HTML Files

Each major journey is its own standalone HTML application with custom capabilities:

  • `vietnam_trip_architect_5_0.html`: 8-day international itinerary, FX currency converter, 9-seater limousine matrix, pure veg dining, Vietnamese audio cards.
  • `chikmagalur_trip_architect.html`: 3-day weekend hill itinerary, 4x4 off-road jeep guide, 125cc scooty hill power rules, Tresca 9:30 AM arrival strategy, Kannada driver address card.

3. How to Add Your Next Trip

Whenever you plan a new trip (e.g. *Goa, Ladakh, Europe*), choose either:

  • Method A (One Click in Browser): Click "+ Add Trip" in the top bar, fill in the details, and it immediately appears in your Hub.
  • Method B (New HTML Companion): Copy `trip_template.html` to `your_trip.html`, add your custom map pins & timeline, and add one entry to `trips_registry.js`.
